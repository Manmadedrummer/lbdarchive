Lethal By Default
Metal • Galesburg, IL
Artist Profile »
In this day and age when pop-rock, boy bands, and hip hop dominate the airwaves and music television, Lethal By Default's thick blend of brains and brawn is giving the hard rock and heavy metal scene a much needed charge. They are an explosive, unrestrained force that have been blessed with an astonishing songwriting sense, fearless innovation, exceptional technical ability and an endless pool of raw talent and creativity. Despite the youth factor, this group from Woodhull, Illinois is becoming widely known around the area for their complex song structures and sophisticated lyrics that pose deep questions about justice, retribution, and political corruption. No doubt the group is inarguably a growing attraction, and with their 2009 release of their first EP 'Shedding Illusions', they are definitely showing that they carve their own path, and are quickly establishing themselves as a proponent to change the face of heavy metal for the better.
