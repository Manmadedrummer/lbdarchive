Graven-Suicide

Woodhull, IL

The Band
Local Band Network Member Since: April 2, 2007
Music types: Metal, Punk

Description:
Graven-Suicide was formed in 2003 by Cory McDowell and Shay Thayer. In 2004 Jason joined the band on drums. Graven-Suicide then changed their name to Subject To Change, but returned to Graven-Suicide in late 2006 when Nick Garcia joined on vocals to complete the line-up.
