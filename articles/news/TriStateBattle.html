Galesburg band wins Tri-State Battle

Staff Writer
Galesburg Register-Mail
Updated Sept. 10, 2009, 9:17 a.m. CT
Share to Facebook Share to Twitter Share by email

Three Galesburg area bands participated in the Tri-State Battle of the Bands Aug. 30 at the Sidelines Sports Bar in Fort Madison, Iowa.

Only Four Remain, one of the Galesburg bands, won, while another area outfit, Silence Before Suicide, took second prize in the 10-band battle. Lethal by Default, from Galesburg, also participated.
