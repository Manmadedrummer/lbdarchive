Poker run party features 5 bands

Staff Writer
Galesburg Register-Mail
Updated July 29, 2010, 12:52 p.m. CT
Share to Facebook Share to Twitter Share by email

Five bands, five cards and one lucky winner will be featured at Billiards On Main's Rock 'N' Roll Poker Run party at 7 p.m. Saturday.

Fans will be issued one playing card after each band plays, and at the end of the night, the highest poker hand will win a merchandise package from all the bands that play that night. The bands playing the event are 3rd Temple from the Quad City area, Lethal By Default from Galesburg, Suckerpunched from the Galesburg area, Chiefton 3 from Galesburg and Verdict from Champaign-Urbana.

Doors open at Billiards on Main, 240 W. Main St., at 7 p.m. and the show starts at 8 p.m. All ages are welcome. Cover is $5.
