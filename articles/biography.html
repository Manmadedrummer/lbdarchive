Biography

In this day and age when pop-rock, boy bands, and hip hop dominate the airwaves and music television, Lethal By Default's thick blend of brains and brawn is giving the hard rock and heavy metal scene a much needed charge. They are an explosive, unrestrained force that have been blessed with an astonishing songwriting sense, fearless innovation, exceptional technical ability and an endless pool of raw talent and creativity. Despite the youth factor, this group from Woodhull, Illinois is becoming widely known around the area for their complex song structures and sophisticated lyrics that pose deep questions about justice, retribution, and political corruption. No doubt the group is inarguably a growing attraction, and with their 2009 release of their first EP 'Shedding Illusions', they are definitely showing that they carve their own path, and are quickly establishing themselves as a proponent to change the face of heavy metal for the better.

In 2003, at the blossoming age of thirteen, guitarist Cory McDowell and bassist Shay Thayer united to form a group called Graven-Suicide. After playing with a handful of other musicians, the core was solidified upon the addition of drummer Eric Gates two years later. They continued to perform under the name Graven-Suicide until 2007 when they changed the name to Lethal By Default - after being influenced by a Lamb of God poster. In 2009 the group added the forth and final member, guitarist Jim Lauver, to complete the current lineup.

Their pedal to the metal velocity, wildly chaotic backdrop, and powerful musical chops paint an effectively chilling background for their peristent manifestation of songs that reflect anger, despair and death. With cuts like 'Dementia' and 'One More Day', their entire performance speaks to a wide-range of people by its denouncement of governmental confusion and long-term effects of psychotic isolation.

This band really knows how to get it done, and with their constant desire to learn, the sky is truly the limit. They are a fantastic and talented group of men who want their message to be heard, and move their crowds in a most intelligent way.
